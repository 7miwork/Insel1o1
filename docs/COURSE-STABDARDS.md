# Course Standards

Every lesson in Insel 1o1 should follow a consistent structure.

## Required Sections

### 1. Introduction

Brief explanation of the lesson.

### 2. Learning Objectives

Students should understand:

- Objective 1
- Objective 2
- Objective 3

### 3. Learning Content

Main educational content.

### 4. Learning Video

Video section or placeholder.

### 5. Mission

Practical task to complete.

### 6. Quiz

Knowledge check.

### 7. Reward

Completion reward.

### 8. XP

Experience points awarded.

### 9. Localization

All lesson content must support i18n.

## Recommended Structure

Introduction
↓
Video
↓
Learning Objectives
↓
Lesson Content
↓
Mission
↓
Quiz
↓
Reward
↓
Next Island

## Completion Criteria

A lesson is considered complete when:

- Mission completed
- Quiz passed
- XP awarded
